# Battlecard: PixelForge

> **Use when:** selling Nimbus Studio against PixelForge, or briefing sales/exec.

**What PixelForge is:** enterprise AI creative suite for large brands — orchestrates
third-party models, ships teams/SSO/SOC2, demo-gated pricing, strong logo wall.

**Their strengths (don't attack head-on):**
- Enterprise trust: SOC2 Type II, SSO, teams (real, and required by big buyers).
- Broad feature suite and credible logos.

**Where they're vulnerable → how to win:**

| Their weakness | Your play | Proof point to land |
|---|---|---|
| Demo-gated, opaque pricing | Transparent Free/Pro pricing | Show the public pricing page |
| Owns no model (orchestrator) | Brand-model training, your data | Live brand-model demo |
| Heavy for small teams | Faster core loop | Time-to-first-render side-by-side |

**Trap questions to ask a PixelForge-leaning buyer:**
- "Can you see a price before signing an enterprise contract?"
- "Whose model generates your images — and can you train one on your brand?"

**Landmines (where you lose — qualify out or match first):**
- Large brand that needs **SOC2 + SSO today** — Nimbus lacks them; qualify out or roadmap it.

**One-liner:** *"PixelForge is an enterprise suite priced behind a sales call. Nimbus wins
where that hurts — transparent pricing and a brand model that's yours."*
