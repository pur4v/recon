Run **recon** in **Position** mode against: $ARGUMENTS

Confirm authorization first. Goal: work out **who the product is for and how it makes
money** — ideal customer profile & segments, pricing & packaging, credit/quota economics,
and the go-to-market motion.

Read the money pages (pricing, enterprise, in-app billing as your authorized account) and
decode the plan/subscription objects where the app hydrates them — that's ground truth over
the marketing table. Signal-read the ICP from logos, testimonials, vocabulary, SOC2/SSO/
on-prem availability, and whether pricing is public (self-serve) or hidden (sales-led).
Distinguish **stated** ICP from **observed** ICP; label inferences (ACV, segment size) as
inferences; if pricing is demo-gated, say so — never fabricate numbers.

Load `skills/recon/reference/mode-position.md` first (and `mode-decode.md` for the plan
objects). Verify with the `verifier` agent. Offer to persist to
`.recon/notes/<product>-position.md` and feed the competitive-analysis deliverable.
