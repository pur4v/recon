Run **recon** in **Compete** mode on: $ARGUMENTS

Baseline your own product from the Survey/Journey/Decode/Position notes — that's the anchor
column. Then research each competitor from **public sources only** (their site, docs,
pricing) — you are **not** authorized to log into competitors' products; recon drives only
the product you own/are authorized to test.

Fan out one sub-agent per competitor with the same output contract. Normalize into: a
**landscape** table (positioning · ICP · model/tech · pricing visibility · threat), a
**parity checklist** (table-stakes vs differentiator vs gap), and a **one-page battlecard**
(strengths not to attack · vulnerability→play→proof · trap questions · landmines ·
one-liner). Flag defunct/pivoted players, mark demo-gated pricing as such (don't invent
tiers), and keep every "where they're weak" defensible. In AI categories, note the model is
usually not the moat — point the fight at workflow depth, packaging, trust, and price.

Load `skills/recon/reference/mode-compete.md`, `fan-out.md`, and the templates
`assets/competitive-analysis-template.md` + `assets/battlecard-template.md`. Run the
adversarial verify pass before presenting. Offer to persist to
`.recon/competitive-analysis.md` and `.recon/battlecard.md`.
